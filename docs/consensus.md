# Source-gated clearing

Boundary: the publisher declares a bounded ledger and its operating permissions; the contract acquires and interprets those permissions under GenLayer consensus; the deterministic solver records maximum permissible reductions. There is no frontend, caller-provided verdict or physical-delivery claim.

The URL must belong to the immutable publisher repository, use a full 40-hex commit, and name a JSON file under `records/`. Bytes are fetched within both nondeterministic paths and independently SHA-256 checked. JSON normalization preserves every permitted field and rejects duplicate keys, unknown fields, wrong position order, Boolean/fractional/out-of-range amounts and malformed clauses. The full normalized record is supplied to the leader and each validator.

Validators reproduce six substantive permission decisions, compare all six exactly, and separately judge quote relevance and conditional logic against the fetched record. Positive, negative and uncertain outcomes all require agreement. An ALLOW cannot be accepted merely because the leader quote is nonempty or contains a feature name. Different source-supported quotes may be used, but every proposed decision and its anchor must pass the independent relevance judgment. Source failures, malformed model outputs and validator exceptions reject the evaluation.

After agreement, capacity is the original amount for ALLOW and zero otherwise. Let each reduction be nonnegative and no greater than its capacity. Alpha conservation requires `a+b=c+e`; beta requires `c+d=a+f`. Gamma's equation then follows automatically. Enumerating `a,b,c,d` and deriving `e,f` visits every feasible integer cancellation vector. Maximizing the sum of its six entries gives the maximum canceled gross volume. Lexicographic maximization of the vector breaks objective ties. The contract checks that all three original net positions equal their residual counterparts.

This is balance-neutral ledger compression, not payment or profit. An excluded obligation cannot participate in a longer cycle: its capacity is zero in every candidate. The resulting batch includes the entire declared input, source-bound decisions, reductions, remaining amounts, before/after net positions and exclusions. A nonzero excluded UNKNOWN produces REVIEW when nothing can be canceled. If some cancellation is possible, NETTED can coexist with UNKNOWN exclusions; no such position is reduced.

Each accepted batch is append-only and independent. Duplicate hashes are rejected, but source-authored variants are separate hypothetical snapshots. Publisher authority and real debt authenticity are outside this primitive. The eight-batch cap is permissionlessly consumable. Numerical completeness of the solver does not guarantee semantic correctness of the permission oracle.
